# Product Mission

## Problem

Personal voice notes are easy to capture but hard to revisit. Users often record thoughts, ideas, meetings, or reminders, then lose value because the recordings are not transcribed, organized, or searchable.

## Target Users

Professionals and students who record meetings, lectures, interviews, or study sessions and want to search, review, and organize the content later without manually listening through long recordings.

## Solution

Provide a web-based voice memo workspace that records or uploads audio, converts it into searchable text, organizes the notes, and helps users quickly recover the information they captured.
